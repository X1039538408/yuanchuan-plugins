zlib inflate subset
------------------------------------------------------------
The FXS archive reader includes a subset of zlib 1.3.1
(`adler32.c`, `inflate.c`, `inffast.c`, `inftrees.c`, `zutil.c` and the
required headers). Those files were copied from the zlib sources distributed
with Ghidra and are used only for local DEFLATE decompression. `crc32.c` is a
small compatible implementation added for the public CRC-32 entry points.
See `vendor/zlib-inflate/README.txt` for provenance.
